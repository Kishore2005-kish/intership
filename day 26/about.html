Our story

A small nursery that grew with its neighbours

Vriksha began on a Pune terrace with forty pots and a notebook of watering timings. Today we work with growers across the country.

How we work

We send home only what we would keep ourselves.

Every plant spends at least three weeks settling in our shade house before it is listed. If it is not rooted and steady, it does not travel. Our partner nurseries are family-run, paid on delivery, and named on every order slip.

We keep the catalogue small on purpose. Eighty varieties we know deeply serve you better than a thousand we cannot advise on.
