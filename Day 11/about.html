Our Heritage

Born from the Taj Legacy

In 1903, Jamsetji Tata opened the Taj Mahal Palace in Mumbai — a beacon of Indian hospitality that would define luxury dining for over a century. Taj Heritage Kitchen was born from that same vision: to celebrate India's rich culinary tapestry with uncompromising quality and grace.

Our founder, Chef Vikram Singh Rathore, trained under the legendary chefs of the Taj Group for over two decades before opening this restaurant in 2010. Today, we stand as a tribute to the royal kitchens of India — from the Awadhi dastarkhwan to the coastal flavours of Kerala.

Every spice is hand-selected, every recipe is time-tested, and every guest is treated like royalty — because at Taj Heritage Kitchen, hospitality isn't just a service, it's our birthright.

Our Journey

Milestones of Excellence

1903

The Taj Legacy Begins

Jamsetji Tata opens the Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai — inspiring a century of culinary excellence.

2010

Taj Heritage Kitchen Opens

Chef Vikram Singh Rathore launches the restaurant, bringing Taj-trained expertise to a standalone dining experience.

2015

First Michelin Recognition

Awarded "Best Indian Restaurant" by Food & Wine India, cementing our place among India's culinary elite.

2020

Expansion & Innovation

Opened our private dining room and introduced a chef's tasting menu featuring 12 courses of regional Indian cuisine.

2026

Continuing the Legacy

Celebrating 16 years of serving authentic Indian flavours with the warmth and elegance of Taj hospitality.
