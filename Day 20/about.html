Four decades of building trust

What began as a two-engineer contracting firm in Andheri is today a pan-India construction company — still run on the same principle of doing the work honestly.

Our story

From one site in Andheri to eighteen cities

In 1986, Shri Vasant Deshmukh took a small municipal contract in Mumbai with borrowed shuttering and a team of nine. He insisted on two things that still define us: pay the labour on time, and never hide a number from the client.

Today the second generation runs a company of 1,200 engineers and workers, delivering everything from SRA redevelopment to IGBC-rated IT campuses — with those same two rules intact.

Milestones

How we grew

  • 1986

    Founded in Mumbai with a municipal drainage contract and a team of nine.

  • 1994

    First residential tower completed in Thane; in-house RCC design team formed.

  • 2003

    ISO 9001 certification; expansion into Pune and Nashik markets.

  • 2011

    Bengaluru and Chennai offices opened; first IT park delivered.

  • 2018

    Infrastructure division launched with the Kalyan Road flyover package.

  • 2024

    Crossed 600 completed projects and 48 million sq ft constructed.

Our values

What we refuse to compromise on

Open books

Every rupee is traceable. Clients see procurement rates, wastage and variations in the monthly reconciliation.

Safety before speed

No target justifies an unsafe lift. Daily toolbox talks and third-party audits on every site.

Fair to the workforce

Weekly wages, EPF/ESIC coverage, clean labour housing and skill training for site staff.
