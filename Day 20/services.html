Services that cover the whole build

From the first feasibility sketch to the final snag list, our teams handle every stage in-house — so accountability never gets passed around.

Turnkey civil construction

Single-contract execution of RCC and steel structures, from excavation to handover, for buildings up to 40 floors.

  • Waterproofing and facade systems
  • Finishes, snagging and handover dossier

Design & build

Architecture, structural design and construction under one roof — reducing coordination gaps and shortening timelines by up to 20%.

  • Concept and GFC drawings
  • Value engineering workshops
  • Single-point cost guarantee

Project management consultancy

Owner-side PMC for clients running their own contractors, with independent quality audits and cost control.

  • Tendering and vendor evaluation
  • Programme and cash-flow control
  • Monthly quality and safety audits

MEP & services

Renovation & retrofit
