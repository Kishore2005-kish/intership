The Studio — Est. 1998

A Studio That Sells The Houses It Understands

We did not set out to be a brokerage. We set out to be a studio that happened to sell the houses it understood best. Twenty-five years on, the principle holds.

Our Position

Every residence is measured, drawn and re-drawn before it is listed. The advisor who walks you through it has walked it as an architect would — from the promenade inward.

The studio now numbers fifty — architects, advisors and a stewardship desk — across three continents. The notebook that began as a single ledger has become the register that quietly moves our most significant residences.

We publish the plan, the light study and the material palette. Nothing else.

Four Principles

The disciplines that have held the studio together since its first ledger.

01

Draw before you sell

A residence enters the register only once its plan has been drawn and its light study settled. We sell the house the way we would build it — from the plan out.

02

The quiet register

The most considered sales happen off-market. Our register moves the most significant residences between households who already understand them.

03

Material as memory

We publish a four-family palette for every listing — the most honest portrait of how a residence will age, and the part no photograph quite captures.

04

Stewardship over turnover

A house we sell is a house we will steward. The management desk holds estates to a single standard long after the signature.
