How coupons work

Can I use two coupons together?

One coupon code applies per order. Bank card offers are settled by your bank and may appear as a separate cashback on your statement.

Where do I enter the code?

On the checkout page there is a promo field above the payment options. Paste the code and the discount updates your total instantly.

Why was my code rejected?

The most common reasons are a basket below the minimum value, an expired code, or a category-specific coupon used on an ineligible product.
