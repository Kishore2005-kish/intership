Our story

A kirana store's memory, run with a farmer's calendar.

Bought direct

from the people who grow it

Fresh Kirana started in 2019 with one tempo van in Dadar and a promise: buy from growers' collectives, skip the mandi middlemen, and pass the difference on to the household.

Today we work with 340 farming families across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Himachal Pradesh. Produce is picked in the evening, graded overnight and loaded before six in the morning.

Dals arrive unpolished. Spices are stone-ground in 40 kg batches so nothing sits losing aroma. Ghee is bilona-churned in Anand. Everything is weighed in kilos and grams, and every price is in rupees, GST included.

What we

stand for

Honest pricing

The price on the shelf is the price at checkout. No surge pricing, no packing charge, no small-cart penalty.

Short chains

Two hands between the farm and your kitchen, most days one. Shorter chains mean fresher produce and better farm-gate rates.

Real weights

Everything is weighed after cleaning and trimming, so a kilo of bhindi is a kilo you can cook.

Less packaging

Cloth bags for produce, returnable crates for staples, and a ₹10 credit each time you hand a crate back.
