Which payment methods are accepted?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery in all serviceable PIN codes.

How long does delivery take?

Same-day morning and evening slots in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi and Chennai. Next-day delivery across the rest of our network.

Is there a delivery fee?

Free above ₹499. Below that a flat ₹49 fee applies, shown before you pay.

Yes. Report within 24 hours of delivery and we refund to the original payment method or replace on the next slot.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Every order gets a GST invoice emailed within an hour of dispatch. Add your GSTIN at checkout for business billing.

How fresh is the produce?

Fruits and vegetables are sourced the previous evening from growers' collectives and loaded before 6 AM. Nothing is warehoused for a week.

Can I change my delivery slot?

Yes, until the order is packed. Call support or reply to the confirmation SMS.

Do you deliver outside the four metros?
