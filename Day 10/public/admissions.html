How it works

Five steps to joining Vidyanjali

1. Submit an enquiry

Fill the enquiry form below or visit the admissions desk. You will receive a reference number by email.

2. Campus visit

A guided 45-minute walkthrough with an admissions counsellor, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am - 1:00 pm.

3. Registration form & documents

Submit the completed form with the documents listed alongside and the non-refundable registration fee of Rs. 1,500.

4. Interaction

Nursery to Class II: informal play-based interaction with the child and parents. Class III onwards: a written assessment in English, Mathematics and Science.

5. Offer & fee payment

Offers are emailed within seven working days. Confirm the seat by paying the first term fee within ten days.

Documents required

  • Birth certificate (municipal, attested photocopy)
  • Report card / transfer certificate of the previous school
  • Aadhaar card of the student and both parents
  • Four recent passport-size photographs of the student
  • Residence proof and caste certificate, if applicable
  • Medical record with immunisation and blood group

Important dates

  • Enquiry & registration opens: 1 August 2026
  • Last date for registration: 30 September 2026
  • Interaction / assessment window: 5-18 October 2026
  • Offer letters emailed: from 25 October 2026
  • Session begins: 8 April 2027
