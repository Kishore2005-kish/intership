Established 1998

From two classrooms to a landmark campus

Vidyanjali International School began in 1998 in a rented bungalow on Baner Road with 46 students and seven teachers. The founders, Dr. Madhav Joshi and Smt. Lata Joshi, wanted a school where children could question freely without losing their cultural moorings.

Twenty-eight years later the school occupies a purpose-built six-acre campus, teaches over 1,850 students from Nursery to Class XII, and counts more than 4,000 alumni across engineering, medicine, design, defence, law and the arts.

What has not changed is the size of a classroom conversation — we still cap sections at 30 students so that every child is heard by name, every day.

What guides us

Vision, mission and values

Our Vision

To be a school where academic excellence, ethical clarity and cultural pride grow together — so that our students become thoughtful citizens who improve the communities they enter.

Our Mission

To teach through inquiry rather than instruction, to know each child individually, and to keep learning joyful, inclusive and free from fear.

Integrity

Honest work, honestly acknowledged.

Respect

For people, languages and difference.

Perseverance

Effort valued as highly as outcome.

Service

Seva as a habit, not an event.

Principal's message

"A school should feel like a safe place to be wrong."

Children learn fastest when they are not afraid — of a wrong answer, of a difficult question, of being different. Our first responsibility, therefore, is emotional safety. Academic rigour follows naturally once a child trusts the room they are sitting in.

We assess continuously rather than dramatically, we speak to parents as partners, and we insist that every child finds at least one thing on this campus they are genuinely good at — whether it is calculus, kabaddi or the tabla.

Dr. Anagha Deshpande
Principal · M.Sc., M.Ed., Ph.D. (Education) · 24 years in school leadership
