Welcome to Vidyanjali

An offering of knowledge — since 1998

Vidyanjali takes its name from vidya (knowledge) and anjali (an offering with cupped hands). For over twenty-five years we have offered our students a education that is academically rigorous, culturally rooted and deeply humane.

Our motto, Vidya Dadati Vinayam — knowledge grants humility — shapes everything from how we teach mathematics to how we run our assemblies.

6-acre green campus

Smart classrooms, labs, library, amphitheatre and playfields.

Wellbeing first

Full-time counsellor, nurse and a no-bullying charter.

Why Vidyanjali

Six reasons families choose us

An education designed around the child — challenging, joyful and grounded in values.

Inquiry-led teaching

Concept-first lessons, project work and lab time in every subject from Class III onwards.

Three-language strength

English, Hindi and Marathi taught with equal care, plus Sanskrit and French electives.

Robotics & AI lab

Atal Tinkering-style makerspace with 3D printing, drones and coding from Class V.

Sport for every child

Cricket, football, athletics, kabaddi, chess, yoga and a 25 m semi-Olympic pool.

Arts & culture

Bharatanatyam, tabla, choir, theatre and visual arts as timetabled subjects, not add-ons.

Safe & secure

CCTV coverage, GPS-tracked buses, verified staff and trained first-aid responders.

Campus life

Spaces that invite children to explore

Every corner of the campus is designed for hands-on learning — from the science block to the banyan-shaded reading court.

  • 18,000-title library with a junior reading loft

  • Semi-Olympic swimming pool and a 200 m athletics track
