Learning journey

Four stages, one continuum

Our programme follows the CBSE framework and the NEP 2020 5+3+3+4 structure, with an activity-first approach in the early years and increasing academic depth as students progress.

Pre-Primary

Nursery - Sr. KG

Play-based learning, phonics, number sense, rhymes, storytelling, motor skills and free play. No written homework.

Primary

Classes I - V

English, Hindi, Marathi, Mathematics, EVS, Computer Literacy, Art, Music and Physical Education.

Middle

Classes VI - VIII

Science and Social Science split into disciplines, lab work, coding, Sanskrit or French elective, project-based assessment.

Secondary & Senior

Classes IX - XII

Board-focused teaching, stream selection at Class XI, career counselling and competitive-exam support.

Classes XI & XII

Senior secondary streams

Students choose a stream after a counselling session with parents, based on Class X performance, aptitude testing and interest mapping.

English Core, Physics, Chemistry, and a choice of:

  • Mathematics + Computer Science (engineering track)
  • Biology + Psychology (medical track)
  • Mathematics + Biology (dual track, subject to marks)

JEE / NEET foundation classes are held on campus after school hours.

  • English Core, Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics
  • Optional: Mathematics or Informatics Practices
  • Add-on certificate in financial literacy and GST basics

CA Foundation and CUET guidance provided from Class XI.

  • English Core, History, Political Science, Economics
  • Optional: Psychology, Fine Arts or Legal Studies
  • Model United Nations and debate as credited activities

Portfolio mentoring for design, law and liberal-arts applications.

  • Physical Education and Yoga
  • Work Experience & community service (40 hours a year)
  • Life skills, digital citizenship and career readiness
