Our story

From one trekking group to 14,800 travellers

Wayfare started in 2011 when two guides ran a single Himalayan trek for friends of friends. Word travelled faster than we did. Today we run fixed-departure and tailor-made journeys across 42 countries, still with the same rule: every itinerary is walked by someone on our team before it goes on sale.

We work with local operators on fair, long-term contracts, cap group sizes so guides actually know your name, and publish the full cost up front — no compulsory tipping pools, no surprise entry fees.
