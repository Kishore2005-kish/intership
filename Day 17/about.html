About Medicative

Three decades of careful medicine, built around patients and the people who treat them.

Our Mission

We Provide Assistance in Various Directions

Medicative opened in 1994 with 40 beds and three doctors. Today, 480 beds and more than 700 clinicians later, the founding rule has not changed: explain everything, hurry nothing, treat the patient in front of you.

  • Multidisciplinary review before every major procedure
  • Transparent, itemised estimates given up front
  • Interpreters and patient advocates available free of charge
