132 Years of Heritage

The Western Ghats Sanctuary

Nestled at 4,200 feet in Chikmagalur, Karnataka — where Indian coffee was first planted under mystic canopy mists.

The Legend of 1670

Seven Seeds Smuggled from Mocha

Legend tells of Sufi saint Baba Budan, who braved a pilgrimage to Yemen in 1670. Smuggling seven raw coffee seeds strapped securely to his chest, he planted them in the fertile soil of Chandragiri hills in Karnataka.

From those seven sacred seeds sprang India's specialty coffee culture. In 1892, the Malnad Crest family established our estate in these very hills, preserving sacred shade-growing techniques passed down through generations.

Historical Journey

Milestones of Malnad Crest

1670

The Seven Seeds of Baba Budan

Sufi saint Baba Budan brings raw coffee beans from Mocha, Yemen to Chikmagalur, initiating Indian coffee cultivation.

1892

Foundation of Malnad Crest Estate

The estate planter bungalow is built, establishing 350 acres of shade-grown Arabica and Robusta plantations under rainforest foliage.

1935

Pioneering Monsooned Malabar Process

Custom wooden monsooning sheds were built along the coast to naturally age raw green coffee with ocean monsoon winds.

2026

Direct Single-Origin Artisanal Roastery

Exporting single-origin micro-lots directly from estate homestays to coffee lovers worldwide.

Eco Harmony

Biodiversity Under Canopy

🌿

Inter-Cropped Spices

Coffee shrubs grow alongside Malnad green cardamom vines, black pepper, and cinnamon, infusing the soil with rich aromatic complexity.

🌳

Three-Tier Forest Shade

Tall Silver Oak, Fig, and Rosewood trees form a multi-level umbrella that filters tropical sunlight and protects soil moisture naturally.

Wildlife Corridor

Home to over 80 species of migratory birds, Malabar giant squirrels, and honey bees that naturally pollinate our coffee blossoms.
