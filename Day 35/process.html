The 5-Step Journey

How Great Coffee Is Crafted

01

Selective Hand Picking

Experienced estate harvesters hand-pick only deep crimson, fully ripe coffee cherries across multiple harvest passes.

02

Spring Water Pulping

Cherries are washed in gravity-fed mountain spring streams to ferment and gently separate sweet fruit pulp from parchment.

03

Monsooning & Sun Drying

For Monsooned Malabar AA, green beans are spread in open coastal sheds to absorb humid monsoon sea winds for 12 weeks.

04

Cast Iron Drum Roast

Roasted in small micro-batches using traditional cast iron drum roasters to unlock nutty, cocoa, and spicy aromas.

05

Custom Grind & Pack

Milled to exact micron size for South Indian Filter or Espresso, then nitrogen-flushed for peak fresh crema.
