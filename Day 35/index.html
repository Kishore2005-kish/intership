Shade Grown Biodiversity

Nurtured Under Native Forest Canopies

Unlike sun-drenched monoculture plantations, Malnad Crest coffee trees flourish beneath a lush three-tiered canopy of silver oaks, rosewood, wild jackfruit, and pepper vines in Chikmagalur, Karnataka.

Slow ripening under natural shade develops intense aromatic density, low natural acidity, and deep notes of dark cocoa, toasted hazelnut, and cardamoms.
