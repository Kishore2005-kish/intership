Help

Sign in is disabled in this prototype

No accounts, passwords or payment details are collected. Login and Register are shown only to represent the real product's navigation. Your searches, passengers and demo bookings are stored in this browser only and never leave your device.

What is real and what is demo data

  • Real: station names, station codes, train names and route corridors.
  • Demo: fares, availability, PNR numbers, coach and berth allotment, and live running status.
  • No IRCTC API is used and no booking is submitted to Indian Railways.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible label and a visible keyboard focus ring.
  • Station search works with arrow keys, Enter and Escape.
  • Availability is never colour-only — it always reads as text, e.g. "Waitlist — WL 18".
  • Form errors are announced and focus moves to the first field that needs attention.
  • Layout reflows for small screens without horizontal scrolling.

Frequently asked

Why is only one journey shown per search?

The prototype focuses on the direct-train comparison problem, which is where most passengers spend time.

Can I cancel a demo booking?

Yes — open My Trips and cancel. A demo refund status appears against the booking.
