Heritage

A palace with a story

Lalitha Mahal was commissioned by the erstwhile Maharaja of Mysore to host the palace's most important guests, among them the Viceroy of India.

  • Built in 1931 at the foot of Chamundi Hill, overlooking the Chamundi Hills.
  • An Italianate palazzo with twin ionic columns, domes and a projecting porch.
  • A central dome above marble interiors, period furniture and royal portraits.
  • Terraced and landscaped gardens surrounding the palace.
