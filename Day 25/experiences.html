Heritage

A palace with a story

Lalitha Mahal was built in 1931, commissioned by the erstwhile Maharaja of Mysore to host important guests, including the Viceroy of India.

1931The palace was built

Princely living, kept intact

The palace stands at the foot of Chamundi Hill, overlooking the Chamundi Hills, and today offers an experience of princely living in a real Maharaja's palace.

An Italianate palazzo

  • Twin ionic columns and domes, with a central dome over the building
  • A projecting porch at the entrance
  • Terraced and landscaped gardens
  • Marble interiors, period furniture and royal portraits
  • Ornate interiors throughout the palace
