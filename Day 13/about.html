About the studio

Architecture with purpose.

We build few projects each year, and we stay with every one of them from the first site visit to the last coat of paint.

Our story

Founded on site, not in a boardroom.

FORMA began in 2014 with two architects who were tired of watching drawings lose their intent on site. We hired our own project managers, then our own site team, and eventually became a practice that both designs and builds.

Today the studio works across three countries on private houses, small workplaces and sensitive renovations. The scale changes; the method does not. We start with the site, test the idea in physical models, and detail everything before the first delivery arrives.

Our philosophy

Three commitments we
never compromise on

Material honesty

Wood stays wood, concrete stays concrete. We choose fewer materials and use them well, so buildings age with grace instead of maintenance.

Light before finish

Orientation, openings and shade are resolved before a single surface is chosen. A well-lit room needs very little decoration.

Built as drawn

Design and construction sit in the same team, so decisions on site are made by the people who drew them.

Why FORMA

One contract. One team. One point of responsibility.

  • Fixed-scope pricing agreed before construction begins
  • Weekly progress reports with photos and cost tracking
  • Structural, thermal and acoustic performance modelled in design
  • A ten-year structural warranty on every home we build
