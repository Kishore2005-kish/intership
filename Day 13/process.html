Process

From first sketch to final detail.

Seven stages, each with a clear deliverable, a fixed fee and a decision point before the next begins.

01

Consultation

A focused conversation about how you want to live, what the site offers and what the budget genuinely allows. You leave with a written summary and a realistic cost range.

02

Site Analysis

Survey, orientation, ground conditions, access and local planning constraints. We map sun paths and prevailing wind before drawing a single wall.

03

Concept

Two or three distinct spatial ideas, presented in physical models and plan. We test them against your brief together and choose one to develop.

04

Detailed Design

Structure, envelope, services and joinery resolved into a coordinated set of drawings. Materials are specified and priced, and approvals are submitted.

05

Construction

Groundwork, frame, envelope and services, managed by our own site team on a fixed programme with weekly photo and cost reporting.

06

Finishing

Floors, joinery, lighting and paint. This is where the drawings pay off: every fixing point and reveal was decided months earlier.

07

Handover

Commissioning, snagging, a full building manual and warranties. We return at three and twelve months to check how the building is settling.
