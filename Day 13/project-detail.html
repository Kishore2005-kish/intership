Residential · Case study

Casa Norte

A quiet family house on a sloping northern plot, organised around a sheltered courtyard and a single long roof.

LisbonLocation
2026Completed
3,200Square feet
14Months on site

Project overview

A house that turns its back on the road and opens to the garden.

The plot fell away steeply to the north, so the house steps with it. Bedrooms sit low and quiet at the back; living spaces rise to meet the treeline through a nine-metre run of glazing.

A cross-laminated timber frame let us build the shell in eleven days, which kept the exposed structure dry and the programme short. Concrete appears only where it earns its place: retaining walls, the hearth and the entry stair.

Concept

One roof, two grounds.

Rather than terracing the site, we kept a single continuous roof and let the floor split into two levels beneath it. The result reads as one calm volume from the street and as a layered, generous interior from the garden.

  • Courtyard shelters the entrance from prevailing wind
  • Deep eaves shade south glazing in summer
  • Rainwater collected to the lower garden terrace
