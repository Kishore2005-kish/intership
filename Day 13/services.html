Services

Everything a building needs, from one studio.

Engage us for a single stage or the whole journey. Most clients start with a consultation and stay through handover.

01 · Architecture

Architecture & Planning

Site analysis, concept design, planning submissions and full technical documentation, resolved in physical models as well as on screen.

  • Feasibility and site capacity studies
  • Planning strategy and submissions
  • Technical drawings ready for construction
Discuss a design →

02 · Construction

Residential Construction

Our own site teams build the houses we draw, on a fixed programme with transparent cost tracking from groundwork to handover.

  • Fixed-scope pricing before we break ground
  • Weekly photo and cost reporting
  • Ten-year structural warranty
Request a build quote →

03 · Interiors

Interior Design

Joinery, lighting, surfaces and furniture designed alongside the building so nothing has to be retrofitted later.

  • Bespoke joinery drawn and made to order
  • Layered lighting design and controls
  • Material palettes tested at full scale
Plan an interior →

04 · Renovation

Renovation

Older buildings deserve better than a cosmetic refresh. We renew structure, insulation and services, then add space where it counts.

  • Condition surveys and structural repair
  • Thermal and acoustic upgrades
  • Extensions that respect the original fabric
Review your building →

05 · Management

Project Management

Already have a design? We can run the programme, procurement and site coordination on your behalf, with a single accountable lead.

  • Tendering and contractor selection
  • Programme, cost and risk control
  • Quality inspections at every stage
Hand over the programme →

06 · Advice

Consultation

A focused paid session before you commit: what the site allows, what it will realistically cost, and whether the idea is worth pursuing.

  • Two-hour session with a lead architect
  • Written summary with budget ranges
  • Fee credited if you proceed with us
Book a consultation →
