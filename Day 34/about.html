Our story

Built by attendees who got tired of bad events

Summit.io started as a 60-person meetup in a Bengaluru co-working space in 2018. The founders were engineers who kept paying for conferences that sold keynote slots to sponsors and called it content. So they ran their own, with one rule: the programme committee, not the sales team, picks the talks.

Eight years later that rule still holds. We now produce four flagship conferences a year, a rolling workshop programme, and monthly community nights in twelve cities. Sponsors fund the expo hall and the coffee — never the main stage.
