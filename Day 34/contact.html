FAQ

Quick answers

Can I transfer my ticket?

Yes — name changes are free up to 48 hours before doors open. Email us the new attendee's details.

Do you offer refunds?

Full refunds up to 14 days before the event, 50% up to 72 hours before, and none after that.

Are sessions recorded?

Keynotes and main-track talks are recorded and shared with all pass holders within two weeks.

Is there a student discount?

Every event has a student tier, plus a grant pool for travel support. Bring a valid student ID.
