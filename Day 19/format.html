The shape of
nine days

Small by design

Six travellers. Two vehicles. One host who does not change halfway through. Groups larger than this stop being a journey and start being traffic.

Shimla or Manali to Kaza and back, unhurried

the maximum on any departure, without exception

Seasons

June to September for open passes and barley fields. January to March for the frozen route, snow leopard tracks and a valley entirely to itself.

Day by day

  1. Day 01

    Shimla to Sarahan

    Deodar forest and the first sight of the Sutlej. An evening at the Bhimakali temple, then early sleep.

  2. Day 02

    Sarahan to Kalpa

    Kinnaur begins. Apple orchards give way to rock. Kinner Kailash at first light from the balcony.

  3. Day 03

    Kalpa to Tabo

    The green ends. Entering the cold desert; the thousand-year-old mud monastery at dusk.

  4. Day 04

    Tabo — acclimatisation

    Nothing scheduled. Caves above the village, morning prayers if you wake for them.

  5. Day 05

    Dhankar and Pin

    A fort-monastery on a crumbling spur, then the wide braided floor of the Pin Valley.

  6. Day 06

    Kaza and Key

    The valley's small capital, then the tiered white sprawl of Key gompa above the river.

  7. Day 07

    Langza · Komic · Hikkim

    Fossil beds at 14,500 ft, the world's highest post office, and a letter you will beat home.

  8. Day 08

    Chandratal

    The moon lake over Kunzum La, and a night with no artificial light in any direction.

  9. Day 09

    Descent to Manali

    Down through Atal Tunnel. Oxygen returns before the noise does.

Included

Homestays and monastery guesthouses, all meals, transport in the valley, inner-line permits, oxygen and a first-aid trained host throughout.

Not included

Flights and rail to the trailhead cities, travel insurance, personal gear, and any promise that the weather will cooperate.
