A departure,
not a destination

Twelve thousand feet above the sea, the air thins and the noise goes with it. We do not organise tours of Spiti. We arrange absences — from calendars, from signal, from the obligation to be everywhere at once.

Four seasons,
one silence

Summer opens the Kunzum La. Winter closes it and leaves the valley to itself. We travel in both, and we never travel in numbers.
