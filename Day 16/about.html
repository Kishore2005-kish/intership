| About |

We make clothes meant to be worn until they are yours.

| Philosophy |

Fewer pieces, cut properly.

Lou Leisure began in a two-room studio with a single wool coat and a stubborn idea: that a wardrobe should be built slowly. We work with Italian double-faced wool, Japanese selvedge denim and 18.5 micron merino, and we produce in runs small enough to check every seam.

Nothing is designed for a season. Everything is designed to be reworn.

| 01 |

Fabric first

Mills chosen before sketches. If the cloth isn't right, the piece isn't made.

| 02 |

Small runs

Limited quantities, numbered where relevant, no mid-season markdowns.

| 03 |

Repair, don't replace

Free mending on outerwear for as long as you own it.
