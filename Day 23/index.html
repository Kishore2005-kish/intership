Our story

From the shade of the Ghats to your dabarah

Our family has picked cherries on the slopes above Mudigere since 1974. What began as forty acres under silver oak is now a network of eleven smallholder estates across Karnataka, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh — all shade-grown, all hand-picked, all paid above the mandi rate.

Every batch is roasted in Bengaluru within nine days of milling, ground coarse for the traditional steel filter, and shipped the same week.

01Shade grown

Silver oak and pepper vine canopies keep the cherry ripening slow and sweet.

Twelve kilo drum roasts, profiled by hand, cupped before every dispatch.

The harvest

8,236 kilos, every single month

Cherries come down from eleven estates between December and March, are pulped within six hours of picking, sun-dried on raised beds and rested before they ever meet the drum.

Average SCA cupping score across our 2026 harvest lots, graded independently in Bengaluru.
