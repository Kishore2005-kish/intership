Vanveda started in 2019 in a Bengaluru kitchen, with a kumkumadi recipe written in my grandmother's Malayalam notebook.

The beginning

One notebook, four ingredients

My grandmother made her own oils in Thrissur — saffron from a tin, manjistha from the local vaidyar, sesame pressed down the road. When I moved to Bengaluru I could buy a hundred serums, but none of them smelled like that notebook.

So we started small: one oil-serum, one clay mask, sold at a Sunday market in Indiranagar. Six years later we work with 48 farm partners from Kashmir to Kerala, and every formula is still signed off by an Ayurvedic practitioner before it is signed off by a chemist.

— Ananya Menon, Founder

Sourced in India

Rajasthan

Multani mitti from Barmer and Pushkar rose water distilled in copper degs during the March harvest.

Karnataka

Sustainably farmed Mysore sandalwood and vetiver, plus our Bengaluru formulation lab.

Kerala

Cold-pressed sesame and coconut, and the practitioners who review every classical formula we adapt.

What we stand for

Simple commitments we publish, and audit every year.

Fair, direct sourcing

No middlemen on any botanical. We publish the price we pay per kilo to our farm partners.

Clean by default

No parabens, sulphates, silicones, mineral oil or synthetic dyes. Ever, in any product.

Cruelty free

PETA-certified. Tested on volunteers across four Indian climate zones, never on animals.

Refill, don't rebuy

Glass jars come with ₹100-off refill pouches, and our cartons are FSC recycled board.
