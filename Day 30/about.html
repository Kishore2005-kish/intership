Aakaar / आकार / Form

Indian in origin.
Open in spirit.

2018

A studio born from a wish to make fewer, better things.

Aakaar began in Jaipur with a sketchbook, a borrowed workshop, and a belief that Indian craft belongs in the present—not behind glass.

Today, we work with a close network of carpenters, carvers, cane weavers, and finishers across Rajasthan and North India.

How we work together

Long relationships make better objects.

We develop each piece directly with the people who make it. Prototypes move between sketchbook and workbench until proportion, comfort, and construction agree.

Our workshop partners set their own pace and price. Their knowledge is credited, valued, and carried into every edition.
