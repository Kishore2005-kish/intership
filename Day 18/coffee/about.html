Sourcing

Chikmagalur To
Church Street

We buy green coffee directly from four family-run estates across Chikmagalur and Coorg, paying above the Coffee Board average and returning to the same growers every harvest.

Every bag carries the estate name, altitude and roast date. If it has been on the shelf longer than fourteen days, it goes to staff brews, not to you.

The process

How Your Cup Happens

STEP 01

Cup & Select

Every lot is cupped blind on the estate and again in Bengaluru before we commit a single sack.

STEP 02

Roast Small

Four-kilo drum batches, profiled by hand, logged to the second so the next batch tastes identical.

STEP 03

Brew Fresh

Ground to order, dialled twice a day against a refractometer, poured within thirty seconds.
