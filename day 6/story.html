The Heritage

Velvet & Vine began in a narrow cobblestone alleyway, where the scent of freshly roasted beans first caught the local breeze in the autumn of 2012. Founded by two visionaries with a penchant for slow mornings and old-world aesthetics, the cafe was born from a desire to escape the frantic pace of modern life.

What started as a three-table micro-roastery has grown into a sanctuary for coffee purists. Our original brick walls still bear the marks of those early roasting trials, reminding us daily that true quality cannot be rushed. We honor the traditions of the masters while embracing contemporary techniques that highlight the unique terroir of each harvest.

Today, we continue to curate an environment that feels like a shared living room—a place where conversation flows as smoothly as our signature Velvet roast.
